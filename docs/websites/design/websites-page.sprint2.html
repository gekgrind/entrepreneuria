Why a better website

Customers look you up before they call.

A clear, current website does a lot of the explaining for you. Here is what it does for the kind of work you do.

A good website helps you show up well and get found more easily. It cannot promise rankings, leads or sales, and we will not tell you it can.

  • Handyman & home repair

    A professional first impression

    A neighbor gives someone your name. They search for it before they call. A tidy site that says what you do and where you work gives them a reason to reach out.

  • Residential cleaning

    Your business information in one place

    List your services, service area and how to book. Customers stop guessing what a standard visit covers and what costs extra.

  • Landscaping & lawn care

    Easier estimate requests

    A short request form asks for the basics: what they need, where, and how to reach them. You read it when you are off the mower, not while you are on it.

  • Small contractors & remodelers

    A convenient way to contact you

    Your phone number, email and a request form are easy to find. Customers choose how to reach you, including after hours.

  • Pressure washing

    Show your services and your own project photos

    Before-and-after shots of your jobs, laid out clearly. We use photos you provide. We never use someone else's work.

  • Every local service business

    A stronger foundation for local search

    Clear service pages, your service area and accurate contact details give search engines what they need to understand your business. Results are never guaranteed.

Questions

Plain answers.

Something missing? Send us a note by email and we will reply in writing.

What is included in the monthly price?

The monthly price covers your website, hosting and routine maintenance. Higher packages add tools such as inquiry capture and customer follow-up. What support covers is outlined in your written proposal.

Is there a setup fee?

Yes. Each package has a one-time setup fee, shown on the package card above. The monthly price is separate.

Can I use my existing domain?

In most cases you can keep your current domain and point it to your new website. We confirm the details with you before launch.

Can you help if I do not have a domain?

Yes. We can help you choose a web address that fits your business. Any registration cost and who owns the domain are spelled out in your written proposal.

Can I request changes?

Yes. You review your design before launch and can tell us what you would like changed. How many rounds of changes are included, and how changes after launch work, are outlined in your written proposal.

What if I already have a website?

Add its address to the request form. We will take a look and show you what a new design could look like. Your current site stays as it is until you decide to switch.

Do I need to understand technology?

No. We handle the setup, hosting and routine maintenance. You tell us about your business and approve the result.

Is the website preview free?

Yes. A personalized preview is free, and there is no purchase or commitment required. We review your details and follow up with your preview and next steps.

Can I get started without a phone call?

Yes. Everything starts with the short form below, or an email. If you would like a call, we can schedule one. It is never required.
