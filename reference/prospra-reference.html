Why Prospra Exists

Built by a Founder Who Needed
What Nobody Was Offering

Prospra was born out of frustration. I was building a business and hitting walls that felt impossible to get past alone — the kind of walls that make you consider throwing in the towel. Then I came across something that changed my perspective entirely: Steve Jobs mentored Mark Zuckerberg while he was building Meta. Warren Buffett mentored Bill Gates. Maya Angelou mentored Oprah.

The pattern was clear. Behind nearly every wildly successful founder, there was a mentor. The research backed it up: 93% of self-made millionaires had mentors, and 84% of CEOs say their mentor helped them avoid costly mistakes.

But here's what didn't sit right with me — 95% of people looking for a mentor never find one. That means the people who need guidance the most are the ones least likely to get it. So I built Prospra to change that. What started as an AI mentor became something much bigger: a hands-on partner that works alongside you to make sure you actually reach your goals.

That just doesn't seem right to me — so I wanted to give everyone the benefit of having something to turn to when they need advice, have questions, or are just feeling frustrated and overwhelmed.
— Misti, Founder of Entrepreneuria
