Questions
Founder-First Answers
Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?
Yes. Your plan changes take effect immediately, and you'll be prorated on billing. No contracts, no penalties, no BS.
What's included in the Starter plan?
Starter gives you real access to all four apps in limited form. It's designed to let you explore the full ecosystem before you commit — not a watered-down teaser.
Who are the 6 board members in Directorium?
Six distinct AI personas, each powered by a different model: Axiom (Strategist), Ledger (Capitalist), Velocity (Growth), Forge (Operator), Sentinel (Risk), and Paradox (Contrarian). Growth gives you 3; Pro unlocks all 6.
What are AI Agent credits used for?
Credits power AI-driven actions across the ecosystem — automated outreach sequences, Directorium board sessions, Synceri automation runs, and Architecta AI generation tasks.
Is there a free trial for Growth or Pro?
Start on Starter for free — no card required. When you're ready to grow, upgrade in one click. We also offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on paid plans.
What does "priority performance" mean?
Pro users get front-of-line access to AI processing — faster responses in Directorium sessions, quicker Synceri automation triggers, and reduced wait times across all apps during peak usage.
