## 1. What this Agreement covers

1.1 **The service.** Entrepreneuria Websites, Website Starter: we design, build, publish, host and look after a website for your business. It's a managed service: we do the technical work, and you approve the result.

1.2 **Order Summary.** Your Order Summary (Schedule A) lists your price, start date, what is included, and how to cancel. If the Order Summary and this Agreement conflict, the Order Summary controls for those items.

1.3 **The free homepage concept isn't part of this Agreement.** It's free, and you have no obligation to buy.

## 2. What's included in Website Starter

2.1 Design and build of your website as described in your written proposal: `[OWNER: pages and features, e.g. "up to N pages: Home, Services, About, Contact"]`.

2.2 Hosting of your website on the platform we choose, with a security certificate (SSL). There's no separate hosting fee.

2.3 Routine platform and security updates that we apply to the hosting platform.

2.4 Post-launch content updates: `[OWNER: D-OWN-11, e.g. "small text and photo changes, up to 30 minutes per month; unused time doesn't carry over"]`. Larger changes, new pages or redesigns are quoted separately and done only if you approve the quote.

2.5 **Not included** unless your proposal says otherwise:
- writing all of your content;
- paid stock images;
- domain registration fees;
- email hosting;
- advertising;
- search-ranking guarantees;
- legal pages written for your business (see §7.3);
- online stores or payment processing for your customers.

## 3. How the project works

3.1 **Proposal.** After you approve your homepage concept, we send a written proposal with your price, what's included, and how changes work.

3.2 **Acceptance.** You accept by accepting this Agreement and paying the setup fee (§4.1).

3.3 **Build and review.**
- We build your website and show it to you before it goes live.
- Before launch, you may request changes: `[OWNER: e.g. "up to two rounds of changes within the agreed scope"]`.
- Changes outside the agreed scope are quoted separately.

3.4 **Launch.** We publish your website only after you approve it. The date we publish it is the **Launch Date**.

3.5 **If the project stalls** (D-OWN-13). If we're waiting on you (approval, content, domain access) for **60 days** after we've asked in writing:
- we may close the project by email;
- the setup fee isn't refunded, because work has started (§4.3);
- we may agree to restart later.

**No monthly fee is charged before the Launch Date.**

## 4. Fees and payment

4.1 **Setup fee.**
- A one-time setup fee of **$199**.
- It's due **when you accept your written proposal and this Agreement, after you approve your homepage concept** (D-OWN-21).
- Work begins after it's paid.

4.2 **Monthly fee.**
- **$75 per month**, starting on the **Launch Date** and charged every month on the same day of the month as the Launch Date.
- If that day doesn't exist in a month, the charge falls on the last day of that month.
- The charge recurs automatically until you cancel (§5).

4.3 **Setup fee refunds** (D-OWN-05).
- (a) If you cancel **before we start building**, we refund the setup fee in full.
- (b) Once building has started, the setup fee isn't refundable.
- (c) If we can't deliver the website described in your proposal, we refund it in full.
- Refunds go back to your original payment method within `[OWNER: 10]` business days.

4.4 **Price changes** (D-OWN-22).
- We may change the monthly fee with at least **30 days'** written notice by email.
- If you don't agree, you may cancel before the change takes effect, and you won't be charged the new price.
- We'll never change a fee you've already paid.

4.5 **Taxes** (D-OWN-23). Prices exclude any applicable taxes. If tax applies, it's shown on your invoice.

4.6 **Payment authorization.**
- Payments are collected through our payment provider's secure payment page `[OWNER: GoHighLevel payments / processor name]`.
- By giving your payment details and ticking the agreement box, you authorize us to charge:
  - (a) the setup fee once;
  - (b) the monthly fee each month from the Launch Date until your cancellation takes effect.
- We'll email a receipt for each charge.

## 5. Term, minimum and cancellation

5.1 **Term.** This Agreement starts when you accept it and continues month to month after the Launch Date until either of us ends it.

5.2 **One-month minimum.** The minimum commitment is **one month of service after the Launch Date**: one monthly fee.

5.3 **No long contract.** After the first month, the service continues month to month.

5.4 **How to cancel** (D-OWN-06).
- Email **`[OWNER: inbox]`** or reply to any invoice or receipt with "cancel" and your business name. `[OWNER: add "or use the cancel button in your customer portal" if GHL supports it]`.
- We'll confirm in writing within **2 business days**.
- Your cancellation takes effect at the **end of the monthly period you've already paid for**, but not before the one-month minimum is met.
- After that there are **no further charges**.
- There is **no cancellation fee**.
- Your website stays live until the cancellation takes effect.

5.5 **Failed payments** (D-OWN-10). If a monthly payment fails:
- we'll email you and retry on about days 1, 3 and 7;
- your website stays live for **14 days** after the failed payment;
- after that, we may pause your website, giving **at least 3 days' notice by email** first;
- we'll restore it within 1 business day after payment;
- if a payment is still unpaid **`[OWNER: 30]` days** after it failed, we may end this Agreement by email.

5.6 **Ending by us.** We may end this Agreement:
- (a) with **30 days'** notice by email, for any reason. Any fees paid for the period after the end date are refunded;
- (b) immediately, if your website content is unlawful, infringes someone's rights, or breaks our platform provider's rules, and you don't fix it promptly after we ask.

## 6. Ownership

6.1 **Your content.** You own all content you give us: text, photos, logos, videos, business information. You give us permission to use, copy, edit and publish it only to provide the service.

6.2 **Your domain** (D-OWN-12).
- Your domain name (web address) belongs to you.
- If we register one for you, we register it **in your name** as the registrant. Registration fees are `[OWNER: paid by you directly / passed through at cost]`.
- If you already have a domain, you keep control of it. You give us the access needed to point it to your website.

6.3 **Our materials** (D-OWN-08). We keep ownership of:
- our templates, design systems, code, layouts, tools and know-how;
- anything we created before or outside your project.

While this Agreement is in effect, you may use them as part of your website. We may use the same general designs and techniques for other customers, but never your content or branding.

Custom graphics we create only for you (for example, a graphic built around your logo) belong to you once your fees are paid, **to the extent the law gives anyone ownership of them**.

Copyright may not protect material generated entirely by AI. We don't promise that any particular element of your website is protected by copyright.

6.4 **Your promises about your content.** You confirm that:
- you have the right to give us your content;
- it's accurate;
- publishing it won't break any law or infringe anyone's rights, for example photos you didn't take or don't have a licence for.

6.5 **What you can take with you** (D-OWN-07). If this Agreement ends, and you ask within **30 days** after it ends, we'll give you:
- (a) all the content and images you gave us;
- (b) the text of your website pages as delivered;
- (c) help moving your domain, such as a transfer code or DNS change.

Your website is built on our hosting platform. **The platform build itself can't be transferred** to another provider. `[OWNER: optional — "We can provide a static copy of your pages for $[X]."]`

- You're responsible for making sure your business, your website content and any claims on it comply with the law that applies to you, including privacy, advertising, licensing and industry rules.

- `[OWNER: choose one]` We'll add a privacy page you provide. / We'll add a basic privacy page template, which is not legal advice and which you must review.

7.5 Don't use the website for unlawful, deceptive, or harmful content. Our hosting platform's acceptable use rules also apply.

## 8. Data

8.1 Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy#websites) explains how we handle your information as our customer.

8.2 **Your website visitors' information.**
- If your website collects information from your visitors (for example, a contact form), that information is yours.
- We handle it **only on your behalf**: to run your website and deliver form messages to you. We don't sell it or use it for our own marketing.
- We keep it confidential and protect it with reasonable safeguards.
- When this Agreement ends, we'll delete it, or give it to you on request within 30 days.

8.3 **No sensitive data.** Unless we agree in writing, your website must not be used to collect:
- health or medical information;
- Social Security numbers;
- financial account or card numbers;
- children's information.

8.4 **Security incidents.** If we learn that your website's visitor data has been accessed without authorization, we'll tell you without undue delay and help you respond.

12.1 **Our promise.** We'll provide the service with reasonable skill and care.

12.2 **Disclaimer.** Apart from §12.1, the service is provided "as is". We don't guarantee:
- search rankings, leads, calls or sales;
- that your website will be error-free or uninterrupted.

12.3 **Indemnity.**
- **You** will cover our reasonable losses from third-party claims that your content or your business practices infringe someone's rights or break the law.
- **We** will cover your reasonable losses from third-party claims that material we created (not your content, and not third-party platforms) infringes someone's copyright.
- The party asking for cover must tell the other promptly and allow it to handle the claim.
