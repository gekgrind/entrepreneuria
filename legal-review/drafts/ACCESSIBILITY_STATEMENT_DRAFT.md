## Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use entrepreneuria.io, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or other assistive technology.

**What we do**

- We design and test our pages against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as our target.
- We check color contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, form labels and error messages when we build or change pages. `[OWNER: keep only if this is actual practice]`
- Our pages work without animation when your device asks for reduced motion. `[ENG: verified for the homepage per AGENTS.md; confirm for /services/websites before publishing]`

**Where we are**

We haven't completed a full independent audit, and some parts of the site may not yet fully meet our target. `[OWNER: list known gaps, if any.]`
